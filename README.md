## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I restore my purchases?
Open Subsoi, navigate to Settings or the Upgrade to Pro screen, and tap **"Restore Purchases"**. Your purchase will be automatically validated with Apple.

### How do I manage or cancel my subscriptions?
Subsoi is a tracking and management tool designed to monitor your expenses. To cancel a third-party service, please open your device's **Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions** or manage it through the respective service provider.

## Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 2026

Subsoi values your privacy. We believe that your personal and financial data belongs solely to you.

1. **No Data Collection:** Subsoi does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, financial data, or tracking analytics.
2. **On-Device Storage:** All subscriptions, categories, payment records, and settings remain stored strictly on your local device.
